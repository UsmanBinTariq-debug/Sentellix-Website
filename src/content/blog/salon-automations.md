If you own a salon, you are probably the owner, manager, receptionist, and marketer all at once. Hiring another person is not always the answer. Often, the bottleneck is repetitive work that a well-built automation can handle for a fraction of the cost.

## 1. Missed-call text-back

When a call goes unanswered, an automated SMS goes out within seconds offering to book online. Recovered bookings often pay for the system in week one.

## 2. Appointment reminders

Two-way SMS reminders 24 hours and 2 hours before the appointment. Confirmations update your calendar automatically.

## 3. Review requests

A polite text 2 hours after the appointment with a single tap to leave a Google review. Most salons see a 3-5x increase in review volume.

## 4. Rebook nudges

A 6-week reminder for colour clients, an 8-week reminder for cuts. Personalised, not spammy.

## 5. No-show recovery

When someone no-shows, an automated follow-up offers a discounted slot in the next 48 hours. Many of these get rebooked.

The pattern is clear: automate the predictable, save your human energy for the work that actually needs you.
